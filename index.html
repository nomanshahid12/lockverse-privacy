01Summary

LockVerse is an on-device app locker and private vault. It is built so that your PIN, pattern, biometric data, hidden photos, notes, contacts, and passwords are processed and encrypted locally, inside LockVerse's own private storage on your device. LockVerse does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not send your personal or vault content anywhere.

The only network activity in LockVerse comes from Google Play's own in‑app update and in‑app review prompts, which talk directly to Google Play services under Google's Privacy Policy — not to LockVerse. LockVerse's own code makes no network calls and has nowhere to send data even if it wanted to.

02Information we collect

Information you create inside the app

  • Unlock secret — the PIN or pattern you set to open LockVerse and your locked apps.
  • Biometric unlock — if you turn on fingerprint/face unlock, authentication is handled entirely by Android's own Biometric framework. LockVerse never receives your fingerprint or face data; it only receives a yes/no result from the operating system.
  • Vault content — photos, videos, notes, contacts, and saved logins that you choose to hide inside LockVerse's Vault.
  • App selections & settings — which apps you choose to lock, your chosen theme, language, and Smart Lock preferences (trusted Wi‑Fi networks or Bluetooth devices you add yourself).
  • Intruder snapshots — a front‑camera photo and timestamp captured automatically when someone enters the wrong PIN/pattern, if you have this feature turned on.

Information generated automatically on your device

  • The name of the app currently in the foreground, read continuously in the background so LockVerse can show its lock screen the instant a locked app opens.
  • The list of apps installed on your device, so you can pick which ones to protect.

None of the above is collected onto any server. It is written to local, encrypted, app-private storage on your device and is never transmitted by LockVerse.

04Vault & intruder photos

Files you hide in the Vault (photos, videos, notes, contacts, saved logins) are encrypted before they are written to disk and can only be decrypted inside LockVerse, after you unlock it. LockVerse never scans, indexes, or uploads Vault content, and has no feature that transmits it anywhere.

If you enable Intruder Selfie, a photo is taken with the front camera after a wrong PIN/pattern attempt and stored, encrypted, in the Intruder Log inside the app. These photos stay on your device until you delete them from the Intruder Log; LockVerse never sends them off the device.

05Storage & security

  • Vault files and the app database are encrypted at rest using AES‑256 (SQLCipher for the database, AES‑GCM for files), with keys generated and held inside the Android Keystore — hardware‑backed on supported devices — so the keys themselves never leave secure hardware.
  • LockVerse's app data is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup (allowBackup="false"), so your vault and settings are not copied to any backup service without your own explicit export.
  • Biometric unlock is verified by the Android operating system itself; LockVerse only ever receives a success/failure result, never raw biometric data.

06Backup & export

LockVerse can create a portable backup file (.lvbak) of your Vault on request. You choose where it's saved — local storage, an SD card, or a cloud folder you pick through Android's own file picker — and you choose the backup password. The backup is encrypted with a key derived from that password before it is written; LockVerse itself never sees, stores, or transmits that file. If you lose the backup password, the backup cannot be opened by you or by us.

07Sharing & third parties

LockVerse does not sell personal data, does not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and does not embed any advertising or analytics SDK. The only third‑party service involved is:

  • Google Play Core (In‑App Update & In‑App Review) — used to prompt you, through Google's own UI, to update LockVerse or leave a Play Store review. This communicates directly between your device and Google Play; LockVerse does not route this traffic or see its contents. See Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

LockVerse may disclose information if required to comply with a valid legal obligation, but since no personal or vault data is collected by LockVerse in the first place, there is nothing on our side to disclose.

08Retention & deletion

  • Vault files, notes, contacts, logins, and intruder photos are kept until you delete them inside the app.
  • Uninstalling LockVerse permanently deletes its entire private storage, including the Vault, settings, and Intruder Log, in line with standard Android app removal — because this data was never copied anywhere else.
  • Backup files you exported yourself live wherever you saved them and follow the retention of that storage location, since LockVerse does not control or retain a copy.

09Children's privacy

LockVerse is a general‑audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by local law). LockVerse does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and in any case processes data locally on-device rather than collecting it onto a server. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, contact us and we will assist in addressing it.

10Your rights & choices

  • Access & delete — everything LockVerse stores about you lives in the app itself; you can view, export, or delete it at any time from within the app, or remove all of it at once by uninstalling.
  • Permissions — every permission above is optional for the features it supports and can be revoked at any time in Android Settings → Apps → LockVerse → Permissions; the corresponding feature (e.g. Intruder Selfie, Smart Lock) will simply stop working until it's granted again.
  • Regional rights — if data‑protection laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA apply to you, you may still exercise rights of access, correction, deletion, and portability; because LockVerse holds no copy of your data outside your own device, these requests are fulfilled directly through the app's own delete/export controls, or by contacting us for guidance.
